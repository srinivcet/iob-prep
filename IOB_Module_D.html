1Credit Monitoring — Overview
▸

Three Functions

  • Control — SMA and Slippage management.
  • Monitoring — CAF 1A/1/50, CSS, Renewal, EWS.
  • Audit — Stock Audit, Credit Audit, ASM.

Key Reports from Branches

  • ERI (Exposure Review & Inspection) Statements — monthly for irregular advances.
  • CSS (Continuous Surveillance Statement) — monthly, FB WC ≥ ₹1 Cr.
  • CAF 1 / 1A / 50 — Credit Appraisal Formats, reviewed by higher authority.
  • SMA — daily reporting to CRILC for exposures ≥ ₹5 Cr.

Account Renewal & Verification

  • 100% renewal/review for accounts with limits ≥ ₹1 lakh.
  • Annual review of all fund-based + non-fund-based limits.
  • Monthly stock statements for CC accounts.
  • Quarterly inspection of borrowing units.

CRILC (Central Repository of Information on Large Credits)

  • RBI's central repository since 2014.
  • Captures all borrowers with aggregate exposure ≥ ₹5 Cr.
  • Daily SMA reporting mandatory.
  • Also quarterly submission of large exposures.
  • Enables early detection of stress across banking system.
3Property Valuation Policy (IOB)
▸
  • Single property > ₹5 Cr — Two valuation reports from different empanelled valuers.
  • If multiple properties — second valuation required only for property with value > ₹5 Cr.
  • Variance treatment:
    • ≤ 10% difference — accept lower value.
    • > 10% difference — engage third valuer (borrower's cost); take lowest.
  • Single property > ₹50 Cr: two valuations, one from corporate/reputed firm.
  • Property > ₹50 Cr: additional legal opinion from corporate law firm (in addition to empanelled lawyer).
  • High-value property visit: joint visit by Credit Dept RO + Branch Manager (property ≥ ₹5 Cr).
  • BM captures property location, uploads to IOB SAHAYAK app.
4CAF — Credit Appraisal Format
▸
  • CAF-1: Review of credit sanctions by sanctioning authority to immediate higher.
  • CAF-1A: Mid-review (interim).
  • CAF-50: Review of accounts beyond ₹50 lakh / specific threshold.
  • Digitization: From January 2026 — automated CAF system.
  • Branch to upload sanction note on the very next day of disbursement.
  • Higher authority reviews within specified timeline.
  • Review findings tracked for compliance.
5Stock Audit
▸
  • For borrowers with CC/OD limit above threshold (typically ₹5 Cr aggregate).
  • Frequency: Annual / Half-yearly for larger accounts.
  • Done by Chartered Accountants or empanelled stock auditors.
  • Scope: Physical verification of stocks, reconciliation with books, aging of debtors, insurance coverage.
  • Report placed before sanctioning authority.
  • Adverse findings → immediate review of limits.
6Credit Audit
▸
  • For accounts above threshold (typically ₹5 Cr).
  • Covers: sanction, disbursement, security creation, end-use, monitoring.
  • Annual audit by designated team.
  • Credit Audit Risk Rating assigned.
  • Report to Audit Committee.
7Concurrent Audit
▸

Objective

Substantial reduction in interval between transaction occurrence and its detection.

Coverage

  • Branches with aggregate deposits + advances > ₹50 Cr.
  • All "A" category branches.
  • Treasury, International Banking, Service branches.
  • Credit Sanction Centres (CPCs, LPCs).
  • Processing Centres (Cheque Processing).

Conduct

  • By CA firms OR retired bank officers.
  • Monthly report to AGM/GM concerned.
  • Must cover 100% of transactions in critical areas.
8Risk-Based Internal Audit (RBIA)
▸
  • RBI directive — adopted by banks.
  • Audit based on risk profile of branch/area.
  • Risk categories: Low, Medium, High, Very High, Extremely High.
  • Based on: Business Risk + Control Risk.
  • Periodicity varies: High risk — frequent; Low risk — less frequent.
  • Three Lines of Defence (3LoD):
    • 1st: Branches/offices.
    • 2nd: Compliance, Risk Management departments.
    • 3rd: Internal Audit.

  • Reported to Board + RBI.
  • Action plan for high-risk areas.
10RBI Inspection (ISE)
▸

ISE — Inspection for Supervisory Evaluation

  • Annual by RBI.
  • Issues IRAR (Inspection and Risk Assessment Report).
  • Non-compliance + Risk Mitigation Plan (RMP) communicated.

Compliance Timelines

Observation TypeTimeline
Majority observations in NCRG (Non-Compliance Reporting Group)Within 45 days
Majority observations in IRARWithin 60 days

Procedure

  • Compliance Dept consolidates replies from concerned depts.
  • Replies verified → administrative note to Top Management.
  • After approval → submitted to RBI.
  • Status reported to senior management + Board.
  • Progress monitored monthly.
  • Compliance verified via sampling.
11Inspection Department
▸

Three Lines of Defence

  1. Branch/Operations level (first).
  2. Compliance & Risk Departments (second).
  3. Internal Audit / Inspection (third).

Types of Audit

  • Regular (Statutory): Annual by SCA.
  • Concurrent: Continuous, monthly reports.
  • Snap: Surprise, unannounced.
  • Credit Audit: Large exposures.
  • IS Audit: Information Systems.
  • Management Audit: Overall effectiveness.
  • Revenue Audit: Income leakage, interest, charges.

Rectification Timelines

  • Serious irregularities: within 3 months.
  • Others: within 6 months.
12Early Warning Signals (EWS)
▸
  • Framework for early detection of stress.
  • Signals tracked for high-value accounts (>₹25 Cr typically).

EWS Triggers

  • Overdue in interest/instalment for > 30 days.
  • Cheque return — insufficient funds.
  • Non-submission of stock statement for > 2 months.
  • Non-cooperation in unit visits.
  • Rapid decline in sales / inventory build-up.
  • Diversion of funds suspected.
  • Negative media reports.
  • CIBIL / CRILC alerts.
  • External audit qualifications.

Red Flagged Accounts (RFA)

  • Accounts with EWS triggers.
  • Classified as potential fraud — investigate in 6 months.
  • Reported to CRILC with "Red Flag" tag.
  • If fraud confirmed — reported as Fraud via FMR-1.
13Branch Inspection Process
▸
  • Scheduled annually; frequency based on risk category.
  • Pre-inspection: notice (except snap).
  • Inspection: all areas — operations, credit, forex, cash, locker, compliance.
  • Draft report → branch response → final report.
  • Rating assigned: Satisfactory / Marginal / Un-Satisfactory.
  • Rectification monitored by RO.
14Fraud Monitoring & Reporting
▸
  • FMR-1: Fraud Monitoring Return — ≥ ₹1 Cr, within 21 days of detection.
  • FMR-2: Quarterly Progress Report on frauds.
  • FMR-3: Annual review of frauds.
  • CFR (Central Fraud Registry): RBI central database.
  • Also reporting under PMLA (STR).
  • Internal reporting: Branch → RO → CO → Board.
15Important Definitions & Terms
▸
  • GL Scrutiny: General Ledger balances tallying with subsidiary ledgers.
  • Nostro/Vostro Reconciliation: Forex accounts tallying with correspondent bank.
  • Suspense Reconciliation: Daily zero-sum of suspense accounts.
  • BRS (Bank Reconciliation Statement): Branch vs CO adjustment.
  • EOD (End of Day) checks: Daily closure, system reconciliation.
  • Preventive Vigilance: Measures to prevent fraud (segregation of duties, maker-checker, periodic rotation).
  • Posting Process Verification: Validation of vouchers in CBS.
