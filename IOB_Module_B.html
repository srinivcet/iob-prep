1Deposits — Overview & Classification
▸

Deposits = liability products (bank's obligation to repay). Appear on LIABILITY side of balance sheet.

Two Major Types

  • Demand Deposits — payable on demand: Savings Bank (SB) + Current Account (CA) = CASA
  • Time Deposits — fixed tenure: Fixed Deposits (FD), Recurring Deposits (RD), etc.

Importance of CASA

  • Low-cost funds for the bank.
  • Higher CASA ratio = better margin for the bank.
  • Target ratio typically 40%+.

Deposit Insurance (DICGC)

  • All deposits covered up to ₹5 lakh per depositor per bank.
  • Includes principal + interest.
  • Different capacity accounts = separate cover.
2Savings Bank (SB) Account
▸

Who Can Open

  • Individuals (including minors through guardian; self-operated from age 10).
  • Associations, Clubs, Trusts (not for profit).
  • Executors/Administrators.
  • HUF.
  • Exception for NPOs and Sec 8 companies (earlier Sec 25 Cos Act 1956): allowed to open SB though not individuals.
  • NOT for business/trading purposes.

IOB SB Interest Rates (as per Cir DEP/05/2026-27 dt 08.10.2026)

Balance SlabRateLinkage
Up to ₹1 lakh2.50%Not linked to Repo
Above ₹1 lakh to ₹2000 Cr2.20%Repo linked (Repo − 3.30%)
Above ₹2000 Cr7.00%Repo + 1.50%

Interest Calculation & Credit

  • Daily product basis.
  • Credited quarterly.
  • Deregulated — banks fix own rates since 2011.

Taxation

  • Domestic SB: Interest taxable; Sec 80TTA exemption up to ₹10,000 (resident individuals/HUF). Senior citizens: Sec 80TTB ₹50,000.
  • No TDS on SB interest unless liquid deposit (sweep) facility.
  • NRE: Tax-free.
  • NRO: TDS @ 30%.

Joint Accounts with NRIs (RBI 09.01.2014)

  • Resident can include NRI close relative on "E/S" basis.
  • Treated as resident account; all resident regulations apply.
  • NRI cannot credit own foreign funds / cheques.
  • NRI operates only for resident's domestic payments.

Minimum Balance (AQB — Average Quarterly Balance)

  • Set by bank; non-maintenance charges as per policy.
  • BSBDA (zero-balance) accounts exempt.
  • Senior citizens / Pensioners — usually relaxed/NIL.
3Revamped SB Account Schemes (IOB)
▸

Regular / Classic SB

Standard SB account for individuals. Basic features.

BSBDA — Basic Savings Bank Deposit Account (PMJDY)

  • Zero balance, no minimum.
  • Max 4 free withdrawals per month at own-bank branch/ATM.
  • Free RuPay Debit Card with ₹2 lakh accidental insurance.
  • Overdraft up to ₹10,000 after 6 months (age 18-65).
  • Full KYC required.

BSBDA Small Account

  • Opened without OVD (OVD in process).
  • Balance cap ₹50,000 at any time; credits ₹1 lakh/year; withdrawals ₹10,000/month.
  • Valid 12 months, extendable by 12.
4Current Account & Cash Credit
▸

Current Account

  • For business/trading purposes.
  • No interest (as per RBI).
  • Unlimited transactions, cheque book.
  • Overdraft/Cash Credit facility available.
  • Opened by: Proprietor, Partnership, LLP, Company, Trust, Govt bodies, Co-operative Society, Clubs/Associations.

Cash Credit (CC)

  • Running credit facility against security of stocks/receivables.
  • Interest on daily outstanding.
  • Drawing Power (DP) = Paid Stock (after margin) + Book Debts (after margin).
  • Stock statement — monthly submission.
  • Renewal — annual review.
  • Margin typically 25% on stocks, 25-40% on receivables.
  • Book debts > 90 days usually excluded from DP.

Current A/c for Borrowers with Credit Facilities (RBI Aug 2020)

  • Borrowers with aggregate credit > ₹5 Cr: CA only with the lending bank(s), not elsewhere.
  • Below ₹5 Cr: CA with any bank (self-declaration from borrower).
  • Monitoring under routing of transactions.
5Capital Gains Account Scheme 1988
▸
  • Under Income Tax Act for deposit of capital gains not utilized in stipulated time.
  • Used for claiming exemption under Sec 54, 54B, 54D, 54F, 54G.
  • Two Types:
    • Type A (SB): Withdrawable, SB interest rate.
    • Type B (Term Deposit): Fixed term like FD.
  • Minimum deposit ₹100; no maximum.
  • If not utilized within specified period → treated as income in year of expiry.
  • Withdrawal: Only for intended purpose (purchase of property/bond).
  • No premature closure of Type B except for utilization.
6CASA Schemes (IOB Specific)
▸

IOB Salary Accounts (Classic / Gold / Diamond / Platinum / Premium)

  • For salaried employees of Govt, PSU, Corporates.
  • Minimum balance: NIL.
  • Free services: ATM (unlimited), NEFT/RTGS, SMS alerts, chequebooks.
  • Insurance: PAI (Personal Accident) — varies by tier.
  • TOD (Temporary Overdraft): 2 months' net salary, max 2 times/year.
  • Concessions on retail loans, locker rent.
  • On retirement/VRS/dismissal: converts to regular SB; concessions withdrawn.

Pensioner Account (I & II)

  • Pensioner I: Central Govt + State Govt + Defence pensioners.
  • Pensioner II: PSU + Autonomous body pensioners.
  • No minimum balance.
  • Free services as per scheme.

SB Max / HNI / Superstar / Ultra HNI

  • Tiered by minimum balance/AQB.
  • Premium Debit Cards (Platinum/Select).
  • Locker concession, demat concession.
  • Higher insurance cover.
  • Dedicated relationship manager (HNI+).

IOB Parivaar (Family Savings)

  • Group 2 to 6 individual SB accounts under one Family ID.
  • Eligible: Spouse, Parents, In-laws, Children, Son/Daughter-in-law.
  • Pooled balance benefits — qualify for higher-tier perks.
  • Excludes: frozen, inoperative, negative balance, charge-unrecovered accounts.
  • All accounts must be fully KYC compliant.

Tamil Nadu Govt Employee SB (IOB)

  • For Tamil Nadu Govt employees, PSUs, Boards, Universities, Autonomous bodies.
  • Age: 18-60.
  • Min balance: NIL; spouse joint allowed.
  • Free: unlimited ATM access (own + other); NEFT/RTGS/IMPS; 60 personalized cheques/yr.
  • PAI: ₹120 lakh (Death + TPD + Air Death/TPD); ₹96 lakh (PPD, Air PPD).
  • Term Insurance: ₹10 lakh.
  • TOD: 2 months' net salary; max 2 times/yr.
  • Concessions: Locker 15%; Housing/Retail processing fee 100% waiver.

IOB Vanitha / IOB Naari (Women-focused)

  • For women account holders.
  • Free health check-up / insurance perks.
  • Preferential interest on retail loans.

SB Prime Account (Senior Citizens)

  • Age 60+.
  • Free doorstep banking (if eligible).
  • Additional 0.50% on FDs.
  • No minimum balance penalty.
7Term Deposits (FD/RD)
▸

Tenure Rules

  • Minimum tenure: 7 days.
  • Maximum tenure: 10 years (NRE/NRO: 10 years; FCNR: 1-5 years).
  • Minor TD: with guardian, max tenure as per policy.

Interest

  • Deregulated — banks fix rates. Rates vary by tenure bucket.
  • Senior Citizen premium: +0.50% (resident Indian 60+).
  • Super Senior (80+): usually +0.75%.
  • Staff: +1% over card rate.
  • Compounding: Quarterly (standard FD).

Premature Withdrawal Penalty

  • Normally 1% below applicable rate for the period deposit actually ran.
  • Some schemes: no penalty for small TDs < ₹15 lakh held for min tenure (bank-specific).
  • Death of depositor: usually no penalty.
  • Minimum 7 days — no interest if closed within 7 days.

Loan Against TD

  • Up to 90% of principal (plus accrued interest).
  • Rate: TD rate + 1% (approx).
  • Processing: instant.
  • Lien marked; no premature closure during pendency.

TDS on FD Interest

  • Individual: 10% if interest > ₹40,000/yr.
  • Senior Citizen: 10% if > ₹50,000/yr.
  • No PAN: 20%.
  • 15G (below 60, income below tax) / 15H (60+) — to avoid TDS.

Recurring Deposit (RD)

  • Fixed monthly instalment for fixed period.
  • Minimum: ₹100/month (as per bank).
  • Tenure: 6 months to 10 years.
  • Penalty for missed instalment: usually ₹1 per ₹100 per month.
  • Interest: compounded quarterly like FD.
8Term Deposit Schemes (IOB)
▸

IOB Senior Citizen Deposit

  • 60+ years resident Indian.
  • +0.50% over card rate (super senior 80+: +0.75%).
  • Age proof mandatory.

Tax Saver FD

  • Lock-in: 5 years (minimum).
  • Max investment: ₹1.5 lakh/yr (eligible for Sec 80C).
  • No premature withdrawal.
  • No loan against; no auto-renewal.
  • Interest taxable.
  • Only individuals/HUF.

IOB Flexi RD

  • Variable monthly deposit (core + top-up).
  • Top-up multiples of core instalment.
  • Flexibility for irregular income earners.

IOB Super Save (Sweep Account)

  • SB + auto-sweep into TD when balance exceeds threshold.
  • Reverse sweep when SB balance falls below threshold.
  • Interest at TD rates (higher than SB).

MOD (Money Multiplier Deposit)

  • Fixed Deposit linked to SB.
  • Auto-break into multiples of ₹1,000.
  • Earns at TD rate; liquid like SB.

IOB Reinvestment Deposit

  • Cumulative FD — interest reinvested quarterly.
  • Maturity value includes all compounded interest.
  • Higher effective yield.

Senior Citizen Savings Scheme (SCSS) — Govt Scheme

  • Age 60+ (55-60 if VRS/superannuation; 50+ for defence).
  • Tenure 5 years; extendable by 3.
  • Max: ₹30 lakh.
  • Interest: notified quarterly (~8.2%); paid quarterly.
  • Sec 80C deduction up to ₹1.5 lakh.
9Gold Monetization Scheme (GMS)
▸

Launched 2015 by GoI — objective: mobilize idle gold lying with households/institutions into productive use.

Three Variants

VariantTenureInterestRedemption
STBD (Short Term Bank Deposit)1 to 3 yearsBank-decided (annual)Gold or INR
MTGD (Medium Term Govt Deposit)5-7 yearsGovt-decided (currently 2.25%)Gold or INR
LTGD (Long Term Govt Deposit)12-15 yearsGovt-decided (currently 2.50%)Gold or INR

Key Features

  • Minimum deposit: 10 grams raw gold.
  • No maximum.
  • Only individuals, HUF, Trusts, Mutual Funds, Cos, charitable institutions.
  • Gold tested at CPTC (Collection and Purity Testing Centre), BIS-hallmarked, then deposited.
  • Depositor receives Gold Deposit Certificate (GDC).
  • Premature withdrawal allowed (MTGD: after 3 yrs; LTGD: after 5 yrs) with penalty.

Tax Benefits

  • Interest earned: tax-exempt.
  • Capital gains on redemption: exempt.
  • No wealth tax (currently abolished).

Benefits to Depositor

  • Earn interest on idle gold.
  • No storage cost / risk.
  • Safer than locker.

Benefits to Bank/GoI

  • Reduce gold imports.
  • Channel to productive use (jewellers, etc.).
10NRE / NRO / FCNR Accounts
▸

Non-Resident External (NRE)

  • Currency: INR.
  • Source: Foreign inward remittance only.
  • Fully repatriable (principal + interest).
  • Interest tax-free.
  • Types: SB, CA, FD, RD.
  • Tenure (TD): 1 to 10 years.
  • Joint: With another NRI OR resident close relative on E/S basis only.

Non-Resident Ordinary (NRO)

  • Currency: INR.
  • For local income (rent, dividends, pension, etc.).
  • Repatriation: Up to USD 1 million per financial year (current income freely repatriable).
  • Interest: TDS @ 30%.
  • Can be joint with NRI or Resident Indian (E/S, F/S).
  • Tenure (TD): 7 days to 10 years.

FCNR(B) — Foreign Currency Non-Resident (Bank)

  • Currency: USD, GBP, EUR, JPY, CAD, AUD (bank's choice).
  • Only Term Deposit — 1 to 5 years.
  • Fully repatriable; tax-free.
  • Immune from exchange risk for depositor.
  • Joint: With another NRI only (or with resident close relative — latter is a variant).
  • Minimum: Usually USD 1,000 or equivalent.

On Return to India (Status Change)

  • NRE / FCNR → RFC (Resident Foreign Currency) account. Funds retain character.
  • NRO → Resident SB.
  • Notify bank within reasonable time.
