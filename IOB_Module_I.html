1Cheque Collection Policy
▸
  • Local cheques (CTS Grid): T+1 / T+2.
  • Outstation cheques: 7-14 working days.
  • Delay compensation: SB rate + 2% above FD rate.
  • Positive Pay System (PPS): mandatory for cheques ≥ ₹5 lakh (RBI effective 1 Jan 2021).
  • Drawer submits cheque details online BEFORE presentation.
  • CTS (Cheque Truncation System) — image-based clearing.
  • CTS 2010 standard: watermark, void pantograph, VOID background, MICR.
2Safe Deposit Locker Policy (RBI 2022)
▸
  • Relationship: Lessor-Lessee (changed from bailor-bailee as per RBI 2022).
  • Agreement renewed every 5 years (model agreement format).
  • Rent collection: Quarterly / half-yearly / annual.
  • Advance rent: max 3 years' rent + ₹500 charges collectable.

Allotment

  • Waitlist mandatory + publicly displayed.
  • Priority-based allotment.
  • Only one locker per hirer (preference).

Operation Monitoring

  • If locker NOT operated for 1 year — letter to hirer.
  • If NOT operated for 3 years — notice issued; bank may break open after another year.
  • If rent unpaid for 3 years — bank may break open after 3 months notice.

Nomination

  • Multiple survivors permitted.
  • Minor nominee — guardian required.

Compensation for Loss

  • Bank negligence: up to 100× annual rent.
  • Natural calamity, customer's own fault: NIL.
  • Customer cannot store illegal goods — prohibited.
3PMJJBY & PMSBY
▸

PMJJBY (Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana)

  • Age eligibility: 18-50 years.
  • Sum assured: ₹2 lakh on death (any reason).
  • Annual premium: ₹436 (auto-debited from SB).
  • Enrollment period: June 1 to May 31 (annual).
  • Cover from day 46 (lien period), except accidental death.
  • Cover continues till age 55.
  • Through LIC + other insurance companies.
  • Claim time: within 30 days of death; nominee submits.

PMSBY (Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana)

  • Age eligibility: 18-70 years.
  • Sum assured: ₹2 lakh (accidental death / total permanent disability).
  • Partial permanent disability: ₹1 lakh.
  • Annual premium: ₹20.
  • Through Public Sector + Private general insurers.

Common Features

  • One account per policy (SB linked).
  • Policy year: June 1 to May 31.
  • Premium auto-debit (consent-based).
  • No medical examination required.
4APY (Atal Pension Yojana)
▸
  • Age eligibility: 18-40 years.
  • Must have SB account.
  • Guaranteed pension from age 60: ₹1,000 / ₹2,000 / ₹3,000 / ₹4,000 / ₹5,000 per month.
  • Monthly contribution depends on joining age + pension amount chosen.
  • On subscriber's death → spouse gets same pension; then nominee gets corpus.
  • Earlier: GoI co-contributed 50% of contribution or ₹1,000/yr (whichever lower) for 5 years (for subscribers joined before 31.03.2016).
  • From 1 Oct 2022: Taxpayers NOT eligible for APY.
  • Regulated by PFRDA.
5NPS (National Pension System)
▸

Overview

  • Launched 2004 for govt employees; 2009 for all citizens.
  • Regulated by PFRDA.
  • Age entry: 18-70 years.

Two Tiers

  • Tier 1: Pension account with restricted withdrawal; mandatory for pension benefits; tax benefits eligible.
  • Tier 2: Voluntary account; any withdrawal; no tax benefits.

Exit at 60

  • 60% lump sum — tax-free.
  • 40% annuity (for monthly pension) — taxable.
  • Can be deferred up to age 75.

Tax Benefits

  • Auto Choice: Age-based glide path (E / C / G).
  • Active Choice: Subscriber selects.

CRAs (Central Record Keeping Agencies)

  • Protean (formerly NSDL).
  • KFin Technologies.
  • CAMS.
6NPS Vatsalya (2024)
▸
  • Launched 2024.
  • For MINORS below 18 years — guardian opens.
  • Min contribution: ₹1,000/year.
  • No maximum.
  • Auto-converts to regular NPS Tier-1 at age 18.
  • Can partially withdraw (max 25% after 3 years for education/medical).
  • Investment choice similar to NPS.
7PPF (Public Provident Fund)
▸
  • Tenure: 15 years, extendable in blocks of 5.
  • Min deposit: ₹500 per year.
  • Max deposit: ₹1.5 lakh per year.
  • Max 12 deposits per year.
  • Interest: notified quarterly (currently ~7.1%); tax-free.
  • Sec 80C deduction up to ₹1.5 lakh; EEE (Exempt-Exempt-Exempt) status.
  • Partial withdrawal: From 7th year, up to 50% of 4th preceding year's balance.
  • Loan: From 3rd to 6th year, up to 25% of 2nd preceding year's balance.
  • One account per person; minor through guardian.
  • No loan against deposit (as collateral for other loans) — specifically prohibited.
8SSY (Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana)
▸
  • For girl child only, up to 10 years of age.
  • Max 2 accounts per family (twins/triplets — exception).
  • Min deposit: ₹250 per year.
  • Max deposit: ₹1.5 lakh per year.
  • Deposit required for 15 years.
  • Maturity: 21 years from date of opening OR on marriage after 18 years.
  • Interest: notified quarterly (currently ~8.2%); tax-free.
  • Sec 80C eligible.
  • Partial withdrawal: 50% for higher education after 18 years.
9SCSS (Senior Citizen Savings Scheme) 2019
▸
  • Age eligibility: 60+ (55-60 if VRS/superannuation; 50+ for defence retirees).
  • Tenure: 5 years; extendable by 3 years.
  • Max deposit: ₹30 lakh (revised from ₹15 lakh in Budget 2023).
  • Interest: notified quarterly (~8.2%); paid quarterly.
  • Sec 80C deduction up to ₹1.5 lakh.
  • Premature closure: after 1 year with penalty.
  • Can be opened at Post Office + nationalised banks.
10Sovereign Gold Bond (SGB)
▸
  • Issued by RBI on behalf of GoI.
  • Denomination: 1 gram gold (and multiples).
  • Min: 1 gram.
  • Max per FY: 4 kg (individual/HUF); 20 kg (trusts).
  • Interest: 2.50% p.a. paid half-yearly on nominal value.
  • Tenure: 8 years; exit after 5th year on interest dates.
  • Maturity redemption: at prevailing gold price.
  • Capital gains at maturity: exempt (for individuals).
  • Interest taxable.
  • Can be used as loan collateral (LTV applies).
  • Issued periodically (tranches).
11KVP & NSC
▸

KVP (Kisan Vikas Patra)

  • Doubles in 115 months (indicative; revisions by GoI).
  • Min: ₹1,000; no max.
  • Not tax-free; no 80C.
  • Can be held in physical certificate or dematerialised.
  • Transferable.

NSC (National Savings Certificate)

  • Tenure: 5 years.
  • Interest: notified quarterly (~7.7%); compounded annually, paid at maturity.
  • Min: ₹1,000; no max.
  • Sec 80C deduction up to ₹1.5 lakh.
  • Interest reinvestment also eligible under 80C (except final year).
  • No premature withdrawal (except death / forfeiture / court order).
12National Savings — MIS / TD / RD
▸

Monthly Income Scheme (MIS)

  • Tenure: 5 years.
  • Min: ₹1,000; Max: ₹9 lakh (single); ₹15 lakh (joint).
  • Interest: notified quarterly (~7.4%); paid monthly.
  • Interest: taxable; no 80C.

Post Office Time Deposit (POTD)

  • Tenures: 1 / 2 / 3 / 5 years.
  • 5-year TD: 80C eligible.
  • Interest payable annually (compounded quarterly).

Post Office RD (5-year RD)

  • Monthly investment for 5 years.
  • Min: ₹100/month.
  • Interest compounded quarterly.
13Insurance Products
▸

Life Insurance Types

  • Term Insurance: Pure life cover; no survival benefit.
  • Endowment: Life cover + savings.
  • Whole Life: Lifetime cover; usually till age 100.
  • Money-Back: Periodic payments + maturity.
  • ULIP (Unit Linked Insurance Plan): Life cover + market-linked investment.
  • Annuity Plans: Income after retirement.

Tax Benefits

  • Sec 80C: Premium up to ₹1.5 lakh.
  • Sec 10(10D): Maturity proceeds tax-free (conditions — premium ≤ 10% of sum assured; 15% for disability).
  • From Apr 2023: ULIPs with premium > ₹2.5 lakh — no 10(10D) exemption.
  • Traditional policies with premium > ₹5 lakh — no 10(10D) exemption (from Apr 2023).

General Insurance

  • Motor: Third-party (mandatory) + Own Damage.
  • Health: Mediclaim, Critical Illness.
  • Property / Fire.
  • Marine (transit).
  • Crop (PMFBY).

Health Insurance

  • Sec 80D deduction: ₹25,000 self+family; +₹50,000 senior citizens parents.
  • Preventive health check-up: ₹5,000 within 80D limit.
  • Pre-existing diseases: 2-4 year waiting period.
14Mutual Funds, Demat, IPO
▸

Mutual Funds

  • Regulated by SEBI.
  • Bank as distributor — AMFI registered.
  • Types: Equity, Debt, Hybrid, ELSS, Index, Sectoral, Thematic.
  • ELSS: 3-year lock-in; Sec 80C eligible.
  • SIP (Systematic Investment Plan) — periodic investment.
  • STP (Systematic Transfer Plan): between MF schemes.
  • SWP (Systematic Withdrawal Plan): regular withdrawal.

Demat Account

  • Depositories: NSDL + CDSL.
  • Required for shares, bonds, SGB, ETFs post dematerialisation.
  • Mandatory for IPO applications in physical shares > 500 shares.
  • CMR (Client Master Report) — DP account details.

IPO

  • ASBA (Application Supported by Blocked Amount) — mandatory.
  • Amount blocked in SB; released on allotment / withdrawal.
  • UPI route for retail investors (₹2-5 lakh).
  • Minimum subscription: 90% of issue (else refund).
  • Price band: Book-built or Fixed-price.
15Pension Accounts Maintenance
▸
  • Bank earns agency commission from GoI/RBI for pension disbursal.
  • Life Certificate submission: Annually (Nov) via physical / Jeevan Pramaan (biometric).
  • Family Pension on pensioner death — spouse eligible.
  • Arrears / DA revision — adjustments.
  • CGHS, income tax deduction, etc.

Agency Commission

  • Pension payments: ₹75 per transaction.
  • Also for Govt business (taxes, deposits).
16Digital Lending Policy 2026
▸
  • RBI Digital Lending Guidelines + updates.
  • LSP (Loan Service Provider): Must be regulated.
  • Disclosure of APR (Annual Percentage Rate).
  • Cooling-off period: cancellation without penalty within specified days.
  • Fair practices + grievance redressal (Internal Ombudsman).
  • Data protection per IT Act + DPDP 2023.
  • DLG (Default Loss Guarantee) framework: cap 5% of loan portfolio.
  • No access to customer's phone contacts, photo gallery (without specific consent).
17Green Finance Framework
▸
  • RBI framework for sustainable finance.
  • Green projects: renewable energy, EV, energy efficiency, waste management, sustainable agriculture.
  • Green Deposits: ESG-aligned; separate accounting.
  • Reporting to RBI quarterly.
  • Sovereign Green Bonds (SGrB) — introduced 2023.
18Interest Rate Policy 2026
▸
  • Board-approved; reviewed annually.
  • Governs rates for all deposits + loans.
  • Rate changes communicated to branches.
  • Compliance with RBI MCLR / EBLR directions.
  • Must be transparent + non-discriminatory.
19Term & Accidental Insurance
▸

Term Insurance (IOB Partners)

  • Pure life cover only.
  • Low premium, high cover.
  • No maturity benefit.
  • Various plans: 10-30 years term.
  • Tax: 80C deduction.

Accidental Insurance

  • Cover: Accidental death + disability.
  • PMSBY: Govt scheme.
  • Individual policies: higher cover.
20Cheque Collection + Health Insurance Products
▸

IOB Health Products

  • IOB Health Care Plus (partnered with Universal Sompo).
  • IOB Jeevan Health.
  • Sum insured options: ₹3 lakh / ₹5 lakh / family cover.
  • Covers hospitalisation, day-care, pre+post hospitalisation.

Mediclaim Features

  • Cashless at network hospitals.
  • Reimbursement at non-network.
  • No Claim Bonus (NCB).
  • Co-pay for senior citizens.
