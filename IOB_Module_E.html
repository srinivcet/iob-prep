1IRAC Norms — Asset Classification
▸

IRAC = Income Recognition, Asset Classification, Provisioning. Recommended by Narasimham Committee.

Asset Classification

CategoryCriteria
StandardNo overdue beyond 90 days; regular interest/instalment service
Sub-StandardFirst 12 months from NPA date
Doubtful 1 (DA-1)Next 12 months (2nd year as NPA)
Doubtful 2 (DA-2)Next 24 months (3rd & 4th year as NPA)
Doubtful 3 (DA-3)5th year onwards
LossIdentified by bank/auditor/RBI as uncollectable

Agricultural NPA Criteria

  • Short-duration crop: NPA if instalment/interest overdue for 2 crop seasons.
  • Long-duration crop: NPA if overdue for 1 crop season.
  • Long-duration crop = crop whose duration exceeds 1 year (sugarcane, banana).

Erosion in Security Value — Direct Classification

  • Realisable value < 50% of outstanding → DIRECT to Doubtful.
  • Realisable value < 10% of outstanding → DIRECT to Loss.

Advances Exempt from NPA Classification

  • Advances against FD (with adequate margin).
  • Advances against NSC (eligible for surrender).
  • Advances against KVP.
  • Advances against LIC Policy.
  • Gold ornaments, govt securities, other securities — NOT exempt.

Consortium Advances

  • Asset classification based on recovery record of each individual bank.
  • Even if other banks are regular, if your bank's instalment is overdue > 90 days = NPA in your books.

Provisioning Coverage Ratio (PCR)

PCR = (Provisions held against NPA + Write-offs) / Gross NPA × 100
Target: generally 70%+ for public sector banks.

Floating Provisions

  • Over and above minimum IRAC requirements.
  • Can be used as buffer against unexpected losses.
  • Cannot be reversed to P&L except RBI permission.
3SARFAESI Act, 2002
▸

Securitisation and Reconstruction of Financial Assets and Enforcement of Security Interest Act, 2002.

Applicability

  • NPA accounts of banks + FIs.
  • Secured loans only.
  • Not applicable to:
    • Agricultural land (specifically exempted).
    • Pledge/lien under Indian Contract Act.
    • Minimum loan outstanding: >₹1 lakh (originally); check latest circular.

Key Sections

  • Sec 13(2): Demand notice — 60 days to repay; classify as NPA.
  • Sec 13(3A): Borrower's objection/representation; bank must reply within 15 days.
  • Sec 13(4): Enforcement — take possession of security (symbolic / physical).
  • Sec 13(8): Right to reconstruction post-sale.
  • Sec 14: Assistance from Chief Metropolitan Magistrate / District Magistrate for physical possession.
  • Sec 17: Appeal to DRT within 45 days of 13(4) action.
  • Sec 18: Appeal to DRAT (Debt Recovery Appellate Tribunal) with 50% deposit of due amount.
  • Sec 20: Central Registry (CERSAI) — within 30 days of charge creation.
  • Sec 29A (IBC-related): Barred persons (wilful defaulters, etc.) cannot buy back under resolution.

Auction Rules

  • Reserve price fixed by valuer.
  • 30 days' notice to borrower + newspaper publication.
  • EMD (Earnest Money Deposit) by bidders: 10% of reserve price.
  • Successful bidder: 25% within specified time (balance 75% within 90 days).
  • Failed auction: can be re-attempted at lower reserve price.

Thresholds

  • Loan outstanding: ≥ ₹1 lakh (historical; newer rules may apply).
  • Agricultural land: NOT covered.
  • Stock/book debts: Can be taken but process different.
4DRT & DRAT
▸
  • DRT (Debt Recovery Tribunal): Specialised forum under RDDBFI Act 1993.
  • Monetary jurisdiction: ≥ ₹20 lakh.
  • Faster resolution than regular courts.
  • Recovery Officer executes DRT orders.
  • Number of DRTs: 39 across India.
  • DRAT (DRT Appellate Tribunal): Appeal against DRT.
    • Appeal within 30 days.
    • 50% of debt to be deposited (can be reduced to 25% at tribunal's discretion).
    • 5 DRATs in India.
  • Further appeal: High Court on points of law only.
5Lok Adalat
▸
  • Under Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987.
  • Alternative Dispute Resolution — mutual settlement.
  • Award is deemed decree of civil court — final; no appeal.
  • No court fee; court fee refunded if case was pending.
  • Both parties must agree — compromise.
  • Monetary limit: up to ₹20 lakh at DRT Lok Adalat.
  • Large Lok Adalat / Permanent Lok Adalat: higher limits.
  • Quick disposal of pre-litigation + pending cases.
6OTS / Compromise Settlement Scheme 2026
▸

OTS = One Time Settlement. OCS = One-time Compromise Settlement.

Features

  • For NPA accounts — compromise settlement below principal + interest.
  • Settlement amount: usually higher of:
    • Book outstanding (part), OR
    • Realisable value of security, OR
    • Negotiated amount as per OTS matrix.
  • Upfront payment: 10-25% at approval.
  • Balance in instalments: 3-12 months (max).
  • Sacrifice: written off against provisions.
  • Settled a/c removed from NPA list.

Sanctioning Authority (indicative)

Settlement AmountAuthority
Up to ₹10 lakhBM / RO Scale IV
₹10L to ₹1 CrRegional Head / RO Committee
₹1 Cr to ₹10 CrCentral Office Committee / GMC
Above ₹10 CrBoard Committee (SMAC / BMC)

OTS Benefits

  • Early recovery, better than lengthy litigation.
  • Reduces NPA ratios.
  • Releases capital for productive use.
  • Avoids provisioning costs.

Non-Discretionary OTS (RBI 2023)

  • Board-approved framework.
  • Wilful defaulters allowed under certain conditions (controversial, debated).
  • Minimum 12 months after NPA.
7Debt Recovery Agent (DRA)
▸
  • Empanelled as per RBI Fair Practices Code.
  • Must undergo IIBF DRA certification.
  • Code of conduct enforced.
  • Calls only between 7 AM to 7 PM.
  • No harassment, abuse, threat.
  • Breach → bank liable; borrower can complain to Ombudsman.
  • Agency commission + incentives.
  • Detective / Investigating agencies: separate empanelment for skip-tracing.
8Insolvency & Bankruptcy Code (IBC) 2016
▸

Consolidated code for insolvency resolution of companies, LLPs, partnerships, individuals.

CIRP (Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process)

  • Initiated by: Financial Creditor, Operational Creditor, Corporate Debtor itself.
  • Minimum default: ₹1 Crore (for corporate debtor).
  • MSMEs: separate pre-pack framework at lower threshold.

Timelines

MilestoneTimeline
CIRP initial period180 days from admission
Extension (one-time)+90 days (total 270)
Outer limit (incl. litigation)330 days
MoratoriumAutomatic under Sec 14 — covers legal actions

CoC (Committee of Creditors)

  • All Financial Creditors.
  • Operational Creditors: present but no voting rights (unless FC's are < 25%).
  • Voting threshold: 66% by value for Resolution Plan approval.
  • Appointment of Resolution Professional (RP).

Key Sections

  • Sec 7: Application by Financial Creditor.
  • Sec 9: Application by Operational Creditor.
  • Sec 10: Application by Corporate Debtor itself.
  • Sec 14: Moratorium.
  • Sec 29A: Persons not eligible to submit Resolution Plan — wilful defaulters, NPA > 1 year borrowers, convicted persons, disqualified directors.
  • Sec 30(2): Resolution Plan mandatory contents.
  • Sec 33: Liquidation process.
  • Sec 53: Waterfall of payments at liquidation.

Liquidation Waterfall (Sec 53)

  1. Insolvency resolution process costs + liquidation costs.
  2. Workmen dues (24 months) + secured creditors' debts.
  3. Wages & unpaid dues of employees other than workmen (12 months).
  4. Financial debts owed to unsecured creditors.
  5. Any amount due to Central/State Govt + debts owed to secured creditors who gave up security interest.
  6. Remaining debts + dues.
  7. Preference shareholders.
  8. Equity shareholders.

Pre-pack Insolvency (for MSMEs)

  • Introduced April 2021.
  • Minimum default: ₹10 lakh.
  • Debtor-in-possession; management retains control.
  • Faster: 120 days total (90 days to approve + 30 for AA).
  • Only for MSMEs.
9Look Out Circular (LOC)
▸
  • Permitted under MHA OM dated 27.10.2010.
  • Authorised officials request Bureau of Immigration (BoI).
  • MoF Notification dated 22.11.2018 included MD/CEO of PSBs as authorised officials.

Against Whom

  • Fraudsters.
  • Wilful defaulters.
  • Money launderers.
  • Economic offenders whose departure is detrimental.
  • Cases where ₹25 lakh+ dues involved.

Address

Deputy Director, Bureau of Immigration [BoI], R.K. Puram, New Delhi.

Validity

  • Usually 1 year; extendable.
  • Can be revoked on compliance.
  • Prevents person from leaving India without clearance.
10Wilful Defaulter
▸

Definition (RBI 2015 Master Circular)

A unit/borrower is Wilful Defaulter if any of the following:

  1. Has defaulted in meeting payment/repayment obligations despite having capacity to pay.
  2. Has diverted funds for purposes other than specified.
  3. Has siphoned off funds (not available in borrower's accounts/business).
  4. Has disposed off/removed secured assets without bank's knowledge.

Threshold

  • Outstanding dues ≥ ₹25 lakh.

Procedure

  1. First Identification Committee (bank-level).
  2. Show-cause notice to borrower (15 days).
  3. Hearing.
  4. Review Committee (bank-level) — final decision.
  5. Reporting to CIBIL / RBI / CRILC.

Consequences

  • Barred from fresh credit from any bank for 5 years from date of removal from list.
  • No capital market access.
  • Cannot be promoter in listed companies.
  • Photograph publication: Permitted in newspapers (Karnataka, Madras HC approved).
  • Sec 29A of IBC: disqualified from submitting Resolution Plan.
  • LOC can be issued.
11Policy on Transfer of Loan Exposures (TLE) 2026
▸
  • Based on RBI Master Directions on TLE 2021 (updated).
  • Permits transfer to: Banks, NBFCs, ARCs, FIs, Investment Funds (for stressed assets).

Minimum Holding Period (MHP)

  • Housing / Retail loans: 3 months.
  • Others: 6 months.
  • Not applicable to stressed assets.

Features

  • True sale (title transfer); not credit enhancement.
  • No recourse/guarantee by transferor post-sale.
  • Transfer price: at commercial rate.
  • For NPA/stressed assets: typically to ARCs via SRA.

Asset Reconstruction Company (ARC)

  • Registered with RBI under SARFAESI.
  • Buys NPAs from banks; recovers via settlement, restructuring, sale.
  • SR (Security Receipts): issued by ARC to bank.
  • SR holder can sell to QIBs.
12Stressed Assets Resolution Framework (Prudential 2025)
▸

RBI Prudential Framework for Resolution of Stressed Assets (June 2019, updated).

Early Identification

  • SMA-0 / SMA-1 / SMA-2 classification per Credit Monitoring norms.
  • CRILC reporting (≥ ₹5 Cr).

Review Period

  • 30 days from default (Reference Date).
  • Lenders decide on resolution plan during this period.

Inter-Creditor Agreement (ICA)

  • Signed by all lenders of a borrower.
  • Decision thresholds:
    • 66% by value AND 60% by number.
  • Dissenting creditor can be bought out.

Resolution Plan Implementation

  • Within 180 days from end of review period (so 180 + 30 = 210 days total from default).
  • If delayed:
    • Beyond 180 days of review: additional 20% provision.
    • Beyond 365 days of review: additional 15% more (total 35%).

Options under Resolution Plan

  • Change in ownership.
  • Restructuring / Rescheduling.
  • Sale to ARC.
  • IBC proceedings.
  • Combination of above.

Independent Credit Evaluation (ICE)

  • For resolution plans involving banks of exposure ≥ ₹100 Cr.
  • ICE by Credit Rating Agencies.
  • Only "RP4 or above" rating plans can be implemented.
13MSME Revival & Rehabilitation
▸

RBI Framework for Revival and Rehabilitation of MSMEs (March 2016, updated).

Coverage

  • MSME units with aggregate loan ≤ ₹25 Cr.
  • Stressed accounts identified via SMA signals.

Committee (at RO/CO level)

  • Formed within 5 days of application (self-initiated or on request).
  • Decides viability + corrective action in 30 days of application.

Three Options

  1. Rectification: Interest/EMI postponement; obtaining additional margin.
  2. Restructuring: Reschedulement of principal/interest; moratorium; conversion of debt to equity.
  3. Recovery: If revival not viable, recovery through SARFAESI/DRT.

Timeline

  • Application/trigger → Committee in 5 days.
  • Decision in 30 days.
  • CAP (Corrective Action Plan) implementation.
14Suit-filed / Decreed Accounts
▸
  • Suit filing after serving demand notice.
  • Jurisdiction: Civil Court (small amounts) or DRT (≥ ₹20 lakh).
  • Decree execution: via Recovery Officer (DRT) or Civil Court.
  • Decree limitation: 12 years from decree date.
  • Can attach property, bank accounts, salary (per Income Tax Act exemptions).
15Technical Write-Off vs Prudential Write-Off
▸
  • Technical Write-Off: Only accounting entry; legal rights maintained. Recovery continues.
  • Prudential Write-Off: Full provision + Board approval for write-off; but legal rights intact for recovery.
  • Both do NOT absolve borrower's liability — recovery continues.
  • Written-off accounts tracked in separate register.
  • Recovery from written-off: 100% credit to P&L.
16Non-Cooperative Borrower
▸
  • Borrower not cooperating with resolution/recovery efforts.
  • Reported to RBI; attracts higher provisioning.
  • Additional provisioning: minimum 20% on SS Assets + 100% on DA/Loss Assets.
  • Different from Wilful Defaulter — may or may not have capacity to pay but not cooperating.
17Fraud Classification & Reporting
▸
  • Account classified as fraud if deceitful intent + loss to bank.
  • Report via FMR-1 to RBI within 21 days of detection.
  • Provisioning: 100% of exposure in installments over 4 quarters (recent RBI relaxation for restructured fraud).
  • All classifications in category-wise fraud returns.
  • Section 447 Companies Act — criminal prosecution.
18Recovery Policy 2026 (IOB)
▸
  • Strategy: Early detection + prompt action.
  • Tools:
    • Negotiated settlement (OTS).
    • SARFAESI action.
    • DRT suit.
    • IBC referral (for corporates).
    • Lok Adalat for small accounts.
    • Sale to ARC.
  • DRA engagement for small / retail accounts.
  • Sahaj — recovery through compromise for small retail accounts.
  • Internal portal for monitoring: PACE, Bank Mitra.
19Compromise Sanctioning (CGC Policy)
▸
  • CGC = Compromise Granting Committee.
  • Bank-level committee: General Manager + designated members.
  • Specific matrix for compromise sanctioning per amount.
  • All settlements require minimum sacrifice justification.
  • Beyond specified limit: Board Committee / Management Committee.
