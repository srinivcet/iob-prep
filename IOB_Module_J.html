1RTGS (Real Time Gross Settlement)
▸
  • Real-time, gross settlement per transaction.
  • Minimum: ₹2 lakh. No maximum.
  • 24x7 since 14 Dec 2020.
  • Settlement individually (vs NEFT batches).
  • Charges (Online retail): NIL (RBI directive).
  • Charges (Branch):
    • ₹2L to ₹5L: ₹25 + GST.
    • Above ₹5L: ₹50 + GST.
  • Primarily inter-bank + high-value transactions.
  • UTR (Unique Transaction Reference) generated for each.
2NEFT (National Electronic Fund Transfer)
▸
  • Deferred Net Settlement; batches of transfers.
  • 24x7 since 16 Dec 2019.
  • Half-hourly batches (48 batches per day).
  • No minimum; no maximum amount.
  • Charges (Online retail): Free.
  • Charges (Branch):
    • Up to ₹10,000: ₹2.50.
    • ₹10,001 to ₹1 lakh: ₹5.
    • ₹1 lakh to ₹2 lakh: ₹15.
    • Above ₹2 lakh: ₹25.
  • UTR: unique for each transaction.
3IMPS (Immediate Payment Service)
▸
  • NPCI service. Instant, real-time, 24x7.
  • Maximum per transaction: ₹5 lakh.
  • Uses MMID (Mobile Money Identifier) + Mobile OR Aadhaar + name OR Account + IFSC.
  • Charges: Nominal (around ₹5-15, bank-specific).
  • P2P (Peer-to-Peer) + P2M (Peer-to-Merchant).
4UPI (Unified Payments Interface)
▸
  • NPCI platform. Launched April 2016.
  • Max per transaction: ₹1 lakh (default).
  • Enhanced to ₹5 lakh for:
    • Capital markets (securities).
    • Insurance.
    • Foreign remittance / inward.
    • Hospitals + Education (approved).
  • VPA (Virtual Payment Address) = user@bank.
  • Instant, 24x7.
  • Per-transaction UPI PIN (4-6 digit).

UPI Variants

  • UPI 123Pay: For feature phones (non-smart) — IVR/missed call.
  • UPI Lite: Offline low-value payments. Wallet up to ₹5,000 (up from ₹2,000). Per-txn ₹500.
  • UPI International: Live in Singapore (PayNow), UAE, Bhutan, Nepal, France, Mauritius, Sri Lanka.
  • UPI AutoPay: E-mandate based recurring payments.
  • UPI Credit Line: Access to pre-sanctioned credit via UPI (2023 launch).
5Debit Cards
▸

Networks

  • RuPay (NPCI, India).
  • Visa.
  • MasterCard.

IOB Variants

  • Classic (basic).
  • Platinum.
  • Select / Priority.
  • Business / Corporate.
  • International (globally usable).

Transaction Limits

Card TypeDaily ATMPOS / E-com
Classic₹25,000₹50,000
Platinum₹50,000₹1 lakh
Select₹1 lakh₹2 lakh

Contactless (NFC)

  • Up to ₹5,000 without PIN (RBI; earlier ₹2,000).
  • Above: PIN required.

ATM Charges

  • 5 free transactions at own bank ATM (financial + non-financial) per month.
  • Other bank ATMs: 3 (metro) / 5 (non-metro) free.
  • Beyond: ₹21 + GST per financial txn; ₹10 + GST per non-financial.
6Credit Cards (IOB)
▸
  • IOB variants: Classic, Gold, Platinum, Business, RuPay Platinum.
  • Interest rate: 2.5% to 3.5% per month on revolving credit (~30-42% p.a.).
  • Grace period: 50 days for purchases (credit cycle).
  • Minimum payment: 5% of outstanding (or full) — avoids default.
  • Late payment fee + over-limit fee as per schedule.
  • Reward points + cashback as per product.
  • Cash withdrawal: ~2.5% + GST + interest from day 1.
  • EMI conversion facility for large purchases.

Features

  • Fuel surcharge waiver (up to a limit).
  • Insurance (lost card, personal accident).
  • Airport lounge access (Platinum+).
7FASTag
▸
  • RFID (Radio Frequency Identification) tag for electronic toll payment.
  • NETC: National Electronic Toll Collection — NPCI platform.
  • Mandatory for all vehicles at National Highways toll plazas (since Feb 2021).
  • Linked to prepaid wallet or bank account.
  • Colour-coded by vehicle class:
    • Violet — Car / Jeep / Van.
    • Orange — LCV.
    • Yellow — Tractor / Commercial.
    • Green — Mini Bus.
    • Pink — Truck.
    • Blue — Bus.
  • Minimum top-up: ₹100.
  • Cashback from NETC at toll plazas (incentive to adopt).
  • Also used for: Parking, Fuel at participating stations (expanding).
8IOB Mobile Banking
▸
  • App: IOB Mobile (Android + iOS).
  • Registration: Via Debit Card + ATM PIN OR Branch.
  • Features:
    • Balance enquiry, mini-statement.
    • Funds transfer (NEFT/RTGS/IMPS/UPI).
    • Bill payments (BBPS).
    • FD opening / closure.
    • Card blocking / hotlisting.
    • Cheque book request.
    • Service requests.
    • e-passbook.
  • Security: 2FA (MPIN + OTP).
  • Session timeout: 5 minutes idle.
  • Device binding.
9Internet Banking
▸

Types

  • Retail Internet Banking (individual + joint E/S accounts).
  • Corporate Internet Banking (for businesses, firms, companies).

Features

  • View statement.
  • Funds transfer (same + other bank).
  • Online tax payment (challan).
  • e-TDS / e-filing.
  • Letter of Credit / BG issuance (corporate).
  • Bulk payment / salary processing.

Security

  • User ID + Login Password + Transaction Password.
  • OTP for transactions.
  • Virtual Keyboard.
  • Transaction limit set by customer.
  • IP-based restrictions (optional).
  • Session timeout: 5 minutes.
10UPI QR Code / IOB Pay / BHIM
▸

UPI QR Code

  • Static QR: linked to one merchant account.
  • Dynamic QR: amount pre-filled by merchant.
  • Interoperable: Any UPI app can scan any BHIM QR.

IOB Pay

  • BBPS-integrated bill payment app.
  • Pay utility bills, mobile, DTH, insurance, loan EMIs, LPG.

BHIM

  • NPCI's UPI app.
  • Works with any Indian bank account.
  • VPA creation (e.g., name@upi).

IOB BHIM / BHIM Aadhaar

  • BHIM IOB UPI — IOB-specific UPI service.
  • IOB BHIM Aadhaar — Aadhaar-biometric based (for BC/micro-ATMs).
11AePS — Aadhaar Enabled Payment System
▸
  • Biometric-based transaction (fingerprint).
  • Done at BC (Business Correspondent) / micro-ATM.
  • Services:
    • Cash deposit.
    • Cash withdrawal.
    • Balance enquiry.
    • Mini statement.
    • Aadhaar-to-Aadhaar fund transfer.
  • Customer needs: Aadhaar linked to bank + biometric.
  • Core role in financial inclusion in rural India.
12Point of Sale (PoS)
▸
  • Merchant acquiring — accepts cards for payment.
  • Types:
    • Traditional PoS (counter).
    • mPoS (mobile; works on smartphone).
    • QR PoS / Soft PoS (contactless on merchant's smartphone).
  • MDR (Merchant Discount Rate):
    • RuPay + UPI: NIL for small merchants (< ₹20L annual turnover).
    • Credit cards: 1-3% (negotiated).
    • Debit cards (other networks): 0.4-0.9%.
  • Settlement typically T+1 to merchant.
13BBPS (Bharat Bill Payment System)
▸
  • Operated by NPCI Bharat BillPay Ltd (NBBL).
  • Interoperable bill payment system.

Categories

  • Electricity, Water, Gas (piped + LPG).
  • DTH + Cable TV.
  • Post-paid Mobile + Landline + Broadband.
  • Education fees.
  • Insurance premium.
  • Credit Card payment.
  • Loan EMI.
  • FASTag recharge.
  • Housing Society maintenance.
  • Subscription fees.

Features

  • Instant / near-instant confirmation.
  • Multiple channels: Internet banking, mobile banking, PoS, physical agent, BBPOU.
  • Any customer of any bank / BBPOU can pay any biller.
14WhatsApp Banking + Cyber Security
▸

IOB WhatsApp Banking

  • Verified WhatsApp Business (Blue Tick).
  • Features: Balance, mini-statement, cheque book request, card blocking, nearest branch/ATM.
  • Non-financial services only.

Cyber Security Framework

  • 2FA (Two-Factor Authentication) mandatory for electronic transactions.
  • Cooling period: 24 hours for beneficiary addition + first transaction.
  • OTP, Biometric, PIN.
  • Device binding.
  • Risk scoring for unusual transactions.

Cyber Fraud Prevention

  • Never share OTP, PIN, CVV.
  • Don't click suspicious links.
  • Verify URLs (https + bank domain).
  • Report fraud immediately — 1930 (cybercrime helpline).
  • National Cyber Crime Portal: cybercrime.gov.in.
