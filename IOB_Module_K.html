1RBI (Commercial Banks – Credit Risk Management) Amendment Directions, 2026
▸

Overview

  • Updates to the credit risk framework under Basel III Standardised Approach.
  • Refined risk weights for various categories (housing, MSME, etc.).
  • Updated capital treatment for unrated / high-rated corporate exposures.
  • Introduction of tighter norms on concentration risk and counterparty credit.

Key Changes

  • Minimum rating requirements for corporate exposures emphasised.
  • Unrated corporates > ₹200 Cr — risk weight 150%.
  • Housing Loan LTV slabs reaffirmed; risk weights unchanged broadly.
  • Enhanced scrutiny on NBFC exposures.
  • Updated External Credit Assessment Agency (ECAI) norms.

Impact

  • Capital planning adjustments for banks.
  • Pricing to reflect risk-based approach.
  • Portfolio diversification emphasis.
2Modified Interest Subvention Scheme — KCC (FY 2025-26)
▸

Key Modification

  • Interest subvention cap enhanced to ₹5 lakh (from ₹3 lakh) for KCC loans for agriculture and allied activities.
  • Applies to short-term loans only — 1 year typical.

Rate Build-up

ComponentRate
Base interest rate (indicative)9%
Less: Interest Subvention (to bank from GoI)2%
Effective rate for farmer7%
Less: Prompt Repayment Incentive (PRI)3%
Final effective rate (prompt repayer)4%

Conditions

  • Must be short-term crop loan or allied activities.
  • Prompt repayment within the specified period.
  • KCC customer must be eligible.
  • Subvention claimed by bank from NABARD/RBI.
3RBI (Commercial Banks – Internal Ombudsman) Directions, 2026
▸

Applicability

  • All Scheduled Commercial Banks (except RRBs).
  • NBFC-ND-SI with ≥ 10 branches.
  • Payment System Operators (threshold-based).

Role of IO

  • Review all customer complaints partially or wholly rejected by the bank.
  • Independent of bank management (functionally).
  • Reviews within 20 days of receiving the rejected complaint.

Qualifications

  • Retired General Manager / CGM of scheduled commercial bank with minimum 7 years' experience.
  • Cannot be a current/former employee of the same bank in past 5 years.

Tenure

  • Initial tenure: 3 years.
  • Extendable: up to 5 years total.
  • Cannot be removed arbitrarily — needs Board approval.

Reporting

  • Administratively reports to MD/CEO.
  • Functionally independent.
  • Quarterly report to Customer Service Committee of Board.

5Digital Payments — E-mandate Framework, 2026
▸

Scope

  • Recurring e-mandate based payments (SIP, insurance, utilities, EMI).
  • Through: Debit Card, UPI, Net Banking.

Key Provisions

  • Per-transaction limit: ₹1 lakh without AFA (Additional Factor Authentication) for specific categories — mutual funds, education, insurance.
  • AFA waiver up to threshold.
  • 24-hour pre-debit notification mandatory to customer.
  • Right to cancel mandate within cooling-off period.
  • Customer consent-based opt-in.

Features

  • Transparent disclosure of amount + frequency.
  • Easy cancellation/modification through same channel.
  • Complaint redressal via Internal + Integrated Ombudsman.
6RBI (Commercial Banks — KCC Scheme) Directions, 2026
▸

Enhanced Features

  • Aadhaar-based simplified onboarding.
  • 5-year validity with annual review.
  • Enhanced coverage: Allied agri activities + animal husbandry + fisheries.
  • KCC-A (for allied activities) — separate sub-variant.
  • Digital KCC platform — online application.

Collateral

  • No collateral up to ₹1.60 lakh.
  • With tied-up marketing arrangement: no collateral up to ₹3 lakh.
  • Above: hypothecation of crops + personal guarantee / third-party security.

Interest Subvention

  • Subvention cap enhanced to ₹5 lakh loans.
  • 2% to bank + 3% to farmer on prompt repayment.
  • Effective rate: 4%.

Insurance

  • PAIS: ₹50,000 accidental death / disability.
  • PMFBY: compulsory for loanee farmers on notified crops.
7RBI (Commercial Banks — Responsible Business Conduct) Third Amendment, 2026
▸

Objectives

  • Fair treatment of customers.
  • Transparent pricing + fair disclosure.
  • Grievance resolution SLAs.
  • Customer protection in digital age.

Key Provisions

  • Enhanced disclosure norms:
    • APR (Annual Percentage Rate) disclosure.
    • All charges, fees upfront.
    • Product features in plain language.
  • Complaint resolution: Within 30 days of receipt.
  • Fair collection practices (Fair Practices Code).
  • Anti-fraud safeguards in digital lending.
  • Transparency on interest calculation methodology.
  • Specific standards for vulnerable customers (senior citizens, differently abled).

Monitoring

  • Board-approved Fair Practices Code.
  • Annual compliance report to RBI.
  • Customer Service Committee of Board oversees implementation.
8RBI (Commercial Banks — Interest Rate on Deposits) Second Amendment, 2026
▸

Key Updates

  • Senior citizen (60+) FD premium reaffirmed: up to +0.50% above card rate.
  • Super senior citizen (80+) FD premium: +0.75% (where applicable).
  • Staff deposit premium: up to +1%.

Premature Withdrawal

  • Standardised framework.
  • Penalty: usually 1% below applicable rate for the period deposit actually ran.
  • Specific carve-outs:
    • Death of depositor — no penalty.
    • Small deposits (below specified threshold) — reduced/no penalty.

Rate Deregulation

  • SB rate deregulated (since 2011).
  • TD rates deregulated, but subject to RBI norms on senior citizen + non-resident specific caps.
  • Minimum reset: as per each bank's policy.

NRE / NRO / FCNR Rates

  • NRE / FCNR: cannot be more than domestic rates (RBI directive).
  • FCNR USD: market-linked (LIBOR/SOFR based).
9RBI (Commercial Banks — Miscellaneous) Supervisory Directions, 2026
▸

Scope — Multiple Topics Consolidated

  • Branch licensing norms.
  • Exposure norms (LEF — Large Exposure Framework).
  • Reporting formats + timelines.
  • Customer identification.
  • Cash management.
  • Business continuity planning (BCP).
  • Updated as on October 01, 2026.

Key Thresholds (Reaffirmed)

  • LEF single counterparty: 20% of Tier 1.
  • LEF group: 25% of Tier 1 (+5% exceptional).
  • Large exposure: ≥ 10% of Tier 1.
  • Sum of all large exposures: ≤ 800% of Tier 1.
  • CRAR minimum: 9% + CCB 2.5%.

Operational

  • BCP mandatory with alternate site operations.
  • Monthly fire drill at head office.
  • Pandemic protocols reviewed.
  • Succession planning for critical roles.
10RBI (Commercial Banks — Statutory Audit) Directions, 2026
▸

Appointment Guidelines

  • SCA (Statutory Central Auditor) — appointed by bank; confirmed by RBI.
  • SBA (Statutory Branch Auditor) — allocated by bank.
  • Audit firm eligibility:
    • CA firm with min 15 years' standing.
    • Specific partner strength.
    • Experience in bank audit required.

Rotation Norms

  • SCA: Rotation after 3 years continuous appointment.
  • Cooling-off: 6 years before re-appointment.
  • Prevents audit familiarity + ensures independence.

Audit Coverage Expanded

  • Large advances — mandatory 100% coverage of exposures > ₹10 Cr (or as set).
  • Non-performing assets.
  • Investment portfolio.
  • Off-balance sheet items.
  • IT general controls.
  • Credit documentation.
  • Internal controls.

Reporting

  • Long Form Audit Report (LFAR) to be submitted.
  • Within specific timelines post year-end.
  • Concerns reported directly to Audit Committee + RBI.
11Master Circular — Credit Facilities to SCs & STs
▸

Objectives

  • Priority sector credit flow to SC/ST.
  • Reduce socio-economic disparity.
  • Employment generation.

Targets

  • Sub-target under Weaker Sections: Minimum credit to SC/ST.
  • Target: 15% of total advances to SC/ST (indicative, under weaker section 12%).
  • Reporting under priority sector achievement.

Schemes Covered

  • DRI (Differential Rate of Interest Scheme) — 4% rate.
  • Stand-Up India.
  • NSFDC refinance schemes.
  • NSTFDC (National Scheduled Tribes Finance Development Corporation).
  • State SC/ST Finance Corporations.
  • Credit Guarantee Scheme for SCs (CGSS-SC).

DBT Routing

  • Subsidies via DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer).
  • Aadhaar-linked.
  • Reduced leakage + direct beneficiary access.

Monitoring

  • Lead Bank Scheme (LBS) monitors district-wise.
  • DCC (District Consultative Committee) tracks progress.
  • SLBC (State Level Bankers' Committee) consolidates state-wise.
  • Quarterly reporting to RBI.

Special Provisions

  • Banks required to identify SC/ST borrowers via self-declaration.
  • No discrimination in loan sanction.
  • Rejection of SC/ST loan application needs justification and reporting.
