PART C-1: GENERAL ADVANCES
1Loan Policy Document (LPD) 2026
▸

LPD is the first and foremost document for credit decisions.

Objectives

  • Comply with GOI + RBI guidelines.
  • Basel norms, IRAC norms, LEF (Large Exposure Framework), PSL targets.
  • Timely credit flow + portfolio diversification.
  • Objective credit decisions + post-disbursement follow-up.
  • Revive distressed assets.

Sanctioning Hierarchy

  • Branch Manager (individual power).
  • LPC Head (Loan Processing Centre).
  • Regional Credit Committee (RCC).
  • Central Credit Committee (CCC).
  • Management Committee (MC).
  • Board of Directors.

ANBC Computation

Adjusted Net Bank Credit = Bank Credit - Bills rediscounted with RBI - Interbank + RIDF outstanding + PSLC - Exemptions

PSLC (Priority Sector Lending Certificate)

  • 4 types: General, Agriculture, SF/MF, Micro.
  • Traded on RBI eKuber platform.
  • Validity: till 31 March of issue year.
  • Minimum ₹25 lakh; multiples of ₹25 lakh.
3Financial Statement Analysis
▸

Balance Sheet (Vertical Form per Companies Act Schedule III)

  • Sources of Funds: Share Capital + Reserves & Surplus + Secured Loans + Unsecured Loans + Current Liabilities & Provisions.
  • Application of Funds: Fixed Assets - Depreciation + Investments + Current Assets + Loans & Advances + Misc Exp not written off.

Profit & Loss Account

  • Revenue from Operations + Other Income = Total Revenue.
  • Expenses: COGS + Employee + Finance + Depreciation + Others.
  • Total Revenue - Total Expenses = PBT.
  • PBT - Tax = PAT.

Cash Flow Statement

  • Three activities: Operating + Investing + Financing.
  • Indirect method: start with PAT, adjust non-cash items.

Tangible Net Worth (TNW)

TNW = Equity Capital + Reserves - Intangibles (goodwill, patents, deferred rev exp, accumulated losses, misc exp).

5Working Capital Assessment
▸

Methods

1. Turnover Method (Nayak Committee)

  • For MSE loans up to ₹5 Crore.
  • Working Capital Requirement = 25% of projected annual turnover.
  • Margin from borrower: 5% of turnover.
  • Bank finance: 20% of turnover.
  • Simple and quick appraisal for small units.

2. MPBF (Maximum Permissible Bank Finance) — Tandon Method II

  • For loans above ₹5 Cr (not MSE).
  • MPBF = 0.75 × (Current Assets - Other Current Liabilities)
  • Minimum 25% margin from borrower (long-term sources).
  • Also Method I: 0.75 × WCG (where WCG = CA - OCL). Note: Method II vs I differ in treatment of margin.

3. Cash Budget Method

  • Seasonal / project-based industries: tea, sugar, construction.
  • Projected monthly cash inflows/outflows → peak funding need = WC requirement.

Operating Cycle (Working Capital Cycle)

RM → WIP → FG → Debtors → Cash
Cycle (days) = RM holding + WIP + FG + Debtor - Creditor

Shorter cycle = less WC needed. Longer cycle = more WC = higher borrowing.
6Term Loan Appraisal
▸

Project Appraisal — 7 Feasibilities

  1. Technical — plant & machinery, technology.
  2. Managerial — promoters' credentials, experience.
  3. Commercial — market demand, competition.
  4. Economic — SWOT, government policy.
  5. Financial — projections, IRR, NPV.
  6. Environmental — EIA clearance if applicable.
  7. Social — benefit to community.

Key Financial Metrics

  • DSCR: Min 1.5× (project-level); 1.2× (bank's comfort).
  • IRR: Internal Rate of Return ≥ cost of capital + risk premium.
  • NPV: Must be positive.
  • Payback Period: Ideally < 7 years.
  • Sensitivity Analysis: Sales ↓10%, cost ↑10%; DSCR still ≥ 1.
  • Break-Even Analysis: BEP (% of capacity) < actual capacity.
  • Moratorium: 6-24 months typical (depends on gestation).

Repayment Schedule

  • EMI (Equated Monthly Instalment) or EQI (Quarterly).
  • Ballooning repayments permitted for project viability.
  • Door-to-Door tenor includes moratorium + repayment period.
7Manual of Documentation 2019
▸

Key Charge Types

  • Hypothecation: Equitable charge on movables; possession with borrower. Governed by Transfer of Property Act + SARFAESI.
  • Pledge: Possession with bank (lender). Indian Contract Act Sec 172-181. Example: gold loan.
  • Lien: Right to retain possession until debt is paid. Example: securities deposit.
  • Mortgage: Charge on immovable property. Transfer of Property Act 1882.
    • Equitable Mortgage (EM): Deposit of title deeds. No registration. Only at notified towns.
    • Registered Mortgage: With Sub-Registrar; higher stamp duty.
    • English Mortgage: Absolute transfer with re-conveyance clause on repayment.
    • Usufructuary: Possession to mortgagee, enjoys income till debt repaid.
    • Simple Mortgage: No transfer of possession; right to sell on default.
    • Anomalous: Combination.
  • Assignment: Transfer of right (LIC policy, book debts).
  • Guarantee: Personal guarantee — Indian Contract Act Sec 126-147.

Limitation Period (Limitation Act 1963)

DocumentPeriod
Promissory Note / Simple Debt3 years from date of acknowledgment
Hypothecation Deed3 years
Equitable Mortgage12 years
Decree of Court12 years
Guarantee3 years from due date

Revival Letter (Acknowledgment)

  • Before expiry of 3 years — obtain fresh acknowledgment of debt.
  • Resets limitation period.
  • Also balance confirmation letter.

Stamp Duty

  • State-specific (Schedule I of Indian Stamp Act + State Schedules).
  • Must be adequately stamped at execution.
  • Insufficient stamp = document inadmissible in court until impounded + penalty paid.

CERSAI Registration (30 days)

All security interests to be registered within 30 days (late with additional fee).

10CGTMSE Scheme
▸

Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro & Small Enterprises — set up by GoI + SIDBI.

Coverage

  • Credit facility (fund-based + non-fund-based) up to ₹5 Crore (revised from ₹2 Cr, Jan 2023) per MSE borrower.
  • Without collateral & third-party guarantee.
  • Both new and existing MSEs eligible.

Guarantee Cover %

CategoryCover %
Micro units up to ₹5 lakh85%
Women / SC/ST / NE Region / ZED-certified / Aspirational Districts85%
Others (Micro & Small up to ₹50 lakh)75%
Credit > ₹50 lakh to ₹5 Cr75%
MSE Retail Trade50%

Annual Guarantee Fee (AGF)

  • Slab-wise:
    • Up to ₹10 lakh: 0.37% - 0.45%.
    • ₹10L - ₹50L: 0.55% - 0.75%.
    • ₹50L - ₹2 Cr: 0.85% - 1.10%.
    • ₹2 Cr - ₹5 Cr: 1.20% - 1.35%.
  • + Risk premium (up to 50% above standard rate).

Lock-in & Claim

  • Lock-in: 18 months from last disbursement/approval.
  • Claim settlement: 75% in 1st instalment (on NPA + legal action); 25% balance on conclusion of recovery.

CGFMU (Credit Guarantee Fund for Micro Units)

  • For PMMY (Mudra) loans up to ₹10 lakh.
  • Covers PSBs, Private Banks, RRBs, Co-op Banks.
  • Guarantee: 85% for first ₹50,000; 75% for next ₹50,000 to ₹1 lakh; 50% above ₹1 lakh up to ₹10 lakh (indicative).
11Key MSME Schemes
▸

PMEGP — PM Employment Generation Programme

  • Nodal agency: KVIC. Max project cost: Manufacturing ₹50 lakh; Service ₹20 lakh.
  • Age: 18+. Education: VIII pass (for Mfg > ₹10L / Service > ₹5L).
  • Beneficiary contribution: General 10%; Special 5%.
  • Subsidy (margin money):
    • Urban General: 15%.
    • Rural General: 25%.
    • Urban Special (SC/ST/W/Minorities/NE): 25%.
    • Rural Special: 35%.
  • Repayment: 3-7 years.
  • 2nd loan under PMEGP (upgradation): up to ₹1 Cr Mfg / ₹25L Service after 3 years with full repayment.

Stand-Up India

  • For SC/ST/Women entrepreneurs.
  • Loan: ₹10 lakh to ₹1 Crore.
  • Greenfield only (not upgradation).
  • Sector: Manufacturing/Service/Trading.
  • Margin: 15%; Repayment: 7 years (18 month moratorium).
  • Rate: MCLR + 3% (max).
  • Combined loan: Term loan + WC.

  • Common infrastructure, bulk procurement, shared assets.
  • Lower risk — cluster synergy.

PART C-3: RETAIL LOANS
13Housing Loan
▸

Eligibility

  • Age: 18-60 (salaried); up to 65 (self-employed/professional).
  • Minimum income: ₹20,000/mo (salaried); ₹1.5-3 lakh/yr (self-employed).
  • Co-applicants: spouse, parent, earning children.
  • CIBIL score ≥ 700 generally.

LTV (Loan-to-Value) — RBI Guidelines

Loan AmountLTVRisk Weight
Up to ₹30 lakh90%35% (LTV ≤ 80%) / 50% (LTV > 80%)
₹30 lakh to ₹75 lakh80%35%
Above ₹75 lakh75%50%

Features

  • Max tenure: 30 years or up to 70/75 years of age.
  • Moratorium: up to 36 months (under-construction).
  • Prepayment: No charges on floating rate housing loans (RBI).
  • Takeover from other banks: no penalty.
  • Top-up loan: available after 2 years of regular EMI.

Interest Rate

  • Linked to Repo (RLLR) — mandatory for retail/MSE from Oct 2019.
  • Reset: minimum every 3 months.

Documents Required

  • KYC + income proof (salary slips 3 months, Form 16, ITR 2 years).
  • Property documents: sale deed, approved plan, EC (13 years), tax receipts, NOC.
  • Builder documents (if under construction): approval, commencement certificate.

Tax Benefits

  • Sec 24(b): Interest deduction up to ₹2 lakh/yr (self-occupied).
  • Sec 80C: Principal repayment up to ₹1.5 lakh/yr.
  • Sec 80EE: Additional ₹50,000 for first-time home buyers (loan ≤ ₹35L, property ≤ ₹50L).
  • Sec 80EEA: Additional ₹1.5 lakh (affordable housing).

14Education Loan
▸

Per IBA Model Education Loan Scheme (revised).

Loan Amount

  • Domestic studies: up to ₹10 lakh. No collateral up to ₹7.5 lakh (CGFSEL — Credit Guarantee Fund for Education Loans).
  • Abroad studies: up to ₹20 lakh (or higher for premier institutions).

Margin

  • Up to ₹4 lakh: Nil.
  • Above ₹4 lakh — Domestic: 5%; Abroad: 15%.

Repayment & Moratorium

  • Moratorium: Course period + 1 year OR 6 months after job, whichever earlier.
  • Repayment: Up to 15 years after moratorium.
  • No pre-payment penalty.

Interest Subsidy (CSIS — Central Scheme of Interest Subsidy)

  • Family income < ₹4.5 lakh/yr.
  • Interest subsidy during moratorium — full interest.
  • For professional/technical courses at approved institutions.
  • Processed via Vidya Lakshmi Portal.

Padho Pardesh

  • For minority community students pursuing abroad studies.
  • Interest subsidy during moratorium.

Co-borrower & Security

  • Co-borrower: Parent/guardian mandatory.
  • Up to ₹4 lakh: No collateral.
  • ₹4-7.5 lakh: No collateral (CGFSEL cover).
  • Above ₹7.5 lakh: Third-party guarantee OR tangible collateral.

Interest Rate

  • RLLR + spread.
  • Simple interest during moratorium.
  • 0.5% concession if interest serviced during moratorium.

IOB Scholar

  • Education loan scheme for premier institutions (IIT, IIM, AIIMS, etc.).
  • Higher loan limits, lower rates.
15Vehicle Loan
▸

New Vehicle

  • 2-Wheeler: Margin 10-15%. Max tenure 5 years.
  • 4-Wheeler: Margin 10-15%. Max tenure 7 years.
  • Commercial Vehicle: Separate scheme.

Used Vehicle

  • Vehicle age ≤ 7 years (at end of loan).
  • Margin: 25-30%.
  • Max tenure: 5 years or till vehicle is 10 years, whichever earlier.

Common Features

  • Hypothecation to bank (noted in RC).
  • Comprehensive insurance mandatory.
  • Bank's name as loss payee.
  • CIBIL 700+.
  • EMI/Income ratio ≤ 50%.
16Personal Loan
▸
  • Unsecured — for any legitimate personal need.
  • Max loan: 20× net monthly salary (typical).
  • Tenure: up to 60 months.
  • EMI/Income ratio ≤ 50%.
  • Rate: higher than secured loans (RLLR + 3-4%).
  • CIBIL 700+; employer stability important.
  • Processing fee: 1-2% of loan amount.
  • Prepayment charges may apply.

IOB Specific Personal Loans

  • IOB Clean Loan: For salaried in reputed organisations.
  • IOB Pensioner Loan: For pensioners up to 65 years.
  • Loan Against FD: Up to 90% of FD, lower rate.
  • Loan Against LIC Policy: Up to 85-90% of surrender value.
PART C-4: AGRI & GOVT SCHEMES
17Kisan Credit Card (KCC)
▸

Single credit instrument for all agri credit needs.

Credit Components

  • Short-term crop loan (seasonal needs).
  • Consumption needs.
  • Domestic needs.
  • Post-harvest expenses + marketing.
  • Consumer durables (10%).
  • Term loan for farm equipment/development.
  • Allied activities: dairy, poultry, fisheries (new addition).

Credit Limit Calculation

  • Scale of Finance × Area + 10% post-harvest + 20% consumption + Farm maintenance.
  • Annually increase 10% increment.

Collateral & Hypothecation

  • No collateral up to ₹1.60 lakh (as per RBI).
  • With tied-up marketing arrangement: no collateral up to ₹3 lakh.
  • Hypothecation of crops + lien on insurance.

Interest Rate (Short-term)

  • Base rate: 9% (indicative, varies by bank).
  • Interest Subvention: 2% to bank from GoI.
  • Effective rate for farmer: 7%.
  • Prompt Repayment Incentive: Additional 3%.
  • Effective: 4% for farmer on prompt repayment.
  • Subvention cap raised to ₹5 lakh KCC loans (RBI Dir 2026, up from ₹3 lakh).

Validity

  • 5 years with annual review.
  • Repayment: coincide with harvest + marketing period.

Insurance

  • PAIS (Personal Accident Insurance Scheme): ₹50,000 (death/disability).
  • PMFBY: compulsory for loanee farmers on notified crops.

KCC Digital Lending (RBI 2026)

  • Aadhaar-based onboarding.
  • Simplified application.
  • Enhanced coverage: crops + allied activities + fisheries (through KCC-A).
18PMFBY (Fasal Bima Yojana)
▸

Launched Feb 2016; crop insurance scheme.

Premium Paid by Farmer

Season/TypePremium
Kharif2%
Rabi1.5%
Horticultural / Commercial5%

Balance premium: shared by Centre + State (50-50; 90-10 for NE/hilly).

Compulsory / Voluntary

  • Compulsory for loanee farmers on notified crops.
  • Voluntary for non-loanee farmers.
  • Voluntary scheme (opt-out) introduced 2020.

Coverage

  • All stages: Pre-sowing to Post-harvest.
  • Risks: natural fire, lightning, storms, hailstorms, cyclones, drought, flood, pest attack, landslide.

  • Crop Cutting Experiments (CCE) + weather parameters.
  • Technology-based: satellite imagery, drones (pilot).

Sum Insured

Sum Insured = Scale of Finance (based on crop + district + area).

Claim Settlement

  • Within 2 months of yield data submission.
  • Through insurer; bank credits to farmer's account.
19SHG / JLG / NRLM / NULM
▸

Self Help Group (SHG)

  • 10-20 members (same socio-economic background; mostly women).
  • Formed by NGO/BC/village head; promoted by NABARD.
  • Savings account opened first — 6 months internal savings/lending.
  • Bank credit linkage: 1st credit = 4× savings (mature: 10×).
  • Peer guarantee; no physical collateral.

Joint Liability Group (JLG)

  • 4-10 members (same village, similar occupation).
  • Mutually trusted; peer guarantee only.
  • Loan: up to ₹10 lakh per JLG; ₹1 lakh indicative per member.
  • Grading after 6 months of group formation.
  • Promoted by NABARD.

DAY-NRLM (Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana — National Rural Livelihoods Mission)

  • Formerly Aajeevika.
  • Focus: rural poor women + SHGs.
  • Interest Subvention: 7% effective (base rate reduced).
  • Additional 3% prompt repayment incentive for women SHGs = 4% effective.
  • Revolving Fund: ₹20,000 after 3 months of savings.
  • Community Investment Fund (CIF): ₹2.5 lakh per SHG.
  • Vulnerability Reduction Fund (VRF).

DAY-NULM (Urban)

  • Urban equivalent of NRLM.
  • SEP (Self Employment Programme) — individual loan up to ₹2 lakh (group: ₹10 lakh).
  • Subsidy: 5% to beneficiary, 7% subvention.
  • Slum-dwellers, urban homeless, street vendors.
  • PM SVANidhi — for street vendors (₹10K → ₹20K → ₹50K tranches).
20DRI / SRMS / Other Govt Schemes
▸

DRI (Differential Rate of Interest Scheme)

  • For weaker sections — poorest of poor.
  • Rate: 4% (flat).
  • Loan: up to ₹15,000 + ₹5,000 (housing).
  • Family annual income cap:
    • Rural: ₹18,000.
    • Urban/Semi-urban: ₹24,000.
  • Minimum 2/3rd of DRI advances to SC/ST.
  • Branch target: 1% of previous year's advances under DRI.

SRMS (Self-Employment Scheme for Rehabilitation of Manual Scavengers)

  • One-time cash assistance: ₹40,000.
  • Skill training with ₹3,000/mo stipend + tool kit.
  • Capital subsidy: up to ₹3.25 lakh.
  • For manual scavengers + dependants.

NSFDC (National SC Finance Dev Corporation)

  • For SC community (BPL + 2× DPL).
  • Term loan, education, Mahila Samridhi, Micro Credit Finance.
  • Loan: up to ₹30 lakh (TL).
  • Rate: 4-8% (concessional).

Jewel Loan

  • Pledge of gold ornaments (18-22 carat preferred).
  • Margin: minimum 25% (LTV max 75%).
  • Valuation by empanelled appraiser.
  • Agri Jewel Loan: eligible for interest subvention if up to ₹3 lakh.
  • Tenure: 12 months (agri); 36 months (non-agri).
  • Reserve Price at auction = outstanding + interest + charges.
  • Auction after 2 notices (15 days each).

Financial Inclusion Initiatives

  • PMJDY (Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana) — zero-balance SB with RuPay card.
  • BSBDA framework.
  • BC/BF network.
  • Financial Literacy Centres (FLC).
  • Village adoption scheme.
